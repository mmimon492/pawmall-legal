PawMall Support

Need help with PawMall? Email us at support@bokbok.tech and include your Player ID (Settings → Player ID → Copy). We reply within a few days.

Common questions

I lost my progress. Your game is backed up online. Sign in with the same Apple or Google account in Settings to bring it back on a new device.

I bought something and did not get it. Open the Shop and tap "Restore purchases". If it is still missing, email us with your Player ID and the date of the purchase.

How do I turn off ads? Buy "Remove Ads" in the Shop. Ads you choose to watch for a reward stay optional.

How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete account. This removes your online backup and your account.
