Delete your PawMall account

PawMall, by Mimo Studio

In the game (fastest)

Open PawMall, tap Settings, then "Delete account". This deletes your account and your cloud backup at once and clears the save on your device.

Without the game

Email support@bokbok.tech with the subject "Delete my account" and your Player ID (Settings → Player ID). If you no longer have the game, tell us the name you used and the sign-in you used (Apple or Google). We delete your account and cloud backup within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

Your account, player name and cloud backup (progress, items and the record of purchases made in the game). Technical error messages are kept for up to 90 days, then deleted. Purchases themselves are recorded by Apple or Google under their own policies; deleting your account does not refund them.
