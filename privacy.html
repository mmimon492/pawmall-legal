PawMall Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026 (revised)

PawMall is a casual game in which you run a mall of cat-run shops. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. "We" means Ke Han Boris Lee, the developer of PawMall, who is responsible for this information (the "controller"). Contact: support@bokbok.tech.

What we collect

  • Game progress. Your save (coins, rooms, levels, items, settings and the record of purchases made in the game) is stored on your device and backed up to our cloud storage so it is not lost.
  • An account identifier. Every player gets a random player ID and an anonymous account so the backup works. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, your account is linked to that sign-in.
  • Your name, only if you give it. With Sign in with Apple or Google we ask only for your name (not your email). You can also type or change a display name in Settings. Your name is shown only to you.
  • Basic app information. With the backup we store the app version, platform, last time the game was online, and technical error messages, to keep the game working.

We do not ask for your email address, contacts, photos, precise location or payment details. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your card details.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob after some mini-games and when you choose to watch an ad for a reward. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice in Settings under "Ad privacy choices". Buying "Remove Ads" turns off the ads that play after mini-games.

Optional analytics ("Use information")

In the iPhone and iPad app, the game asks once whether it may use information about how you play. It stays off unless you tap "Allow", and you can change your choice at any time in Settings under "Use information". Your choice does not affect play, rewards or cloud saves.

If you allow it, Google Analytics for Firebase receives:

  • gameplay activity: screens opened, mall level, mini-game levels and their results;
  • in-app purchases and ad revenue events;
  • app and device information (such as app version, device model, operating system and country) and a random app-instance identifier.

We do not send your player name, email, sign-in account or cloud save ID to Analytics. Advertising identifiers and ad personalisation are turned off for Analytics, and this choice is separate from your ad privacy choices. Turning it off stops collection and resets the Analytics data stored on your device; it does not erase reports that were already sent. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/privacy.

Update alerts (push notifications)

In the iPhone and iPad app, the game may ask whether you want a notification when a new version or event is out. It stays off unless you tap "Notify me" and then allow notifications in the system prompt. You can turn it off at any time in Settings under "Update alerts", or in your device's notification settings. Your choice does not affect play, rewards or cloud saves.

If you turn it on, Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) and the Apple Push Notification service receive a push token for your device so the notification can be delivered. We send the same message to everyone who opted in; we do not use the token to identify you, and it is not linked to your player name, sign-in account or cloud save. Turning update alerts off unsubscribes your device and deletes the token.

In the Android app, update alerts are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google). If you turn them on, Google receives a push token for your device, and on Android 13 or later the system asks for your permission to show notifications. Turning update alerts off deletes the token.

How we use it

  • To save and restore your progress on your devices.
  • To deliver items you buy or earn, and to restore purchases.
  • To find and fix problems in the game.
  • If you allow optional analytics, to understand how the game is played and improve it.

We do not sell your information for money and we do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Legal bases (EU and UK players)

  • Saving your progress, your account, purchases and fixing problems: needed to provide the game you asked for (contract).
  • Personalised ads and optional analytics: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.
  • Non-personalised ads and keeping the game secure: our legitimate interest in funding and protecting a free game.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your information, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. Email support@bokbok.tech with your Player ID (Settings → Player ID) and we will reply within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

California residents: we do not sell personal information for money. When personalised ads are on, Google AdMob may use device identifiers and ad data for cross-context advertising, which California law calls "sharing". You can opt out at any time in Settings under "Ad privacy choices"; we do not treat you differently for using any of these rights.

Where it is stored

Cloud backups are stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication). Only your own account can read or change your backup.

Firebase and AdMob are run by Google and may process data in the United States and other countries. Google protects these transfers with the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Data is encrypted in transit.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your backup while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the game: open Settings and tap "Delete account". This deletes your cloud backup and your account, and clears the save on your device. If you signed in with Apple, the game also revokes its access to your Apple ID. "Reset game" in Settings clears only the save on your device. You can also ask us to delete your account and data without opening the game: see Delete your PawMall account. Technical error messages are kept for up to 90 days; Analytics data, if you allowed it, is kept by Google for up to 14 months.

Children

PawMall is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
